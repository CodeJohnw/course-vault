# Low Traffic Neighbourhoods

Low Traffic Neighbourhoods 通过 modal filters、one-way streets、traffic calming 等方式减少穿行机动车交通，并改善步行、骑行和公共空间。

关联课程：[[UCL BPLN0091 Transport Policy and Planning]]、[[University of Leeds TRAN5015M Shaping Future Transport Systems]]。
